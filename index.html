Ryosei Tamura

Undergraduate @ Keio University · Momentum Robotics Lab @ CMU

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at Keio University. I work with Prof. Jeffrey Ichnowski in the Momentum Robotics Lab at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute. My research focuses on robot learning for dexterous manipulation, especially how robots can learn precise, contact-rich skills from human demonstrations.

I joined the lab in summer 2026 as a Robotics Institute Summer Scholar (RISS), sponsored by SoftBank Group Corp. and Arm. Since the program ended, I have continued working with the team remotely from Keio.
